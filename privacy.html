Privacy Policy — Etech Pro

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Abdi Technology ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Etech Pro mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights in relation to that information.

By using Etech Pro, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information collected automatically

When you use the App, the following information may be collected automatically:

  • Device identifiers — Android Advertising ID (AAID), device model, manufacturer, and OS version.
  • Network information — IP address and approximate geographic location derived from IP.
  • App usage data — screens viewed, features used, session duration, and search queries entered in the App.
  • Diagnostic data — crash logs, error reports, and performance metrics.
  • Ad interaction data — ad impressions, clicks, and video completion events recorded by advertising SDKs.

1.2 Information you provide

Etech Pro does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to use the App.

2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used for the following purposes:

  • Delivering and improving the App's features and content.
  • Displaying relevant advertising through third-party advertising partners.
  • Sending push notifications about new content (only if you grant notification permission).
  • Analyzing app performance and diagnosing errors.
  • Fraud prevention and security.

4. Advertising and Your Choices

Advertising partners in this App may use your Android Advertising ID to show you personalized ads based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time:

  • On your Android device go to Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (Android 12+) or Opt out of Ads Personalization (earlier versions).
  • You can also reset your Advertising ID at any time from the same menu.

When you opt out, advertising partners will still show ads but they will not be personalized to your interests.

5. Data Retention

Etech Pro does not maintain its own user database. Saved tips are stored locally on your device only and are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear app data.

Data collected by third-party SDKs is retained according to each provider's own data retention policies. Refer to the privacy policy links in Section 3 for details.

To request deletion of data held by a specific third-party provider, please contact that provider directly using the links above.

6. Log Data

When the App encounters an error, third-party crash reporting tools may collect Log Data including your device's IP address, device name, OS version, app configuration at the time of the error, and the date and time of the error.

7. Cookies

Etech Pro does not use cookies directly. Some third-party advertising and analytics SDKs included in the App may use similar tracking technologies (such as device identifiers and web beacons) to collect and use information.
